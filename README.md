# Charity Netball – Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for the Charity Netball iOS and Android app, published with GitHub Pages from `index.html`.

To publish: Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: `main`, folder `/ (root)`.
The policy will then be available at https://eddcjdavis.github.io/charitynetballprivacy/

Update the effective date at the top of `index.html` whenever the policy changes.
